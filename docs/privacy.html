Svara

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 June 2026

Short version: Svara is private by design. Your practice lives on your device. We don’t run ads, we don’t sell or share your data, we don’t track you across apps, and there is no social feed. You can use the whole app without an account.

No ads No tracking No social feed No account required On‑device data

1. Who we are

Svara (“we”, “us”, “the app”) is a daily spiritual‑wellness app rooted in Hindu culture. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

2. What data the app stores

Svara stores the following on your device only:

  • Your profile: a display name (defaults to “Friend”) and, only if you choose to sign in, an email address.
  • Your progress: practice sessions, streaks, points, completed lessons, observed festivals and unlocked achievements.
  • Your preferences: reminder times and notification choice.
  • Private reflections: any notes you write in a reflection prompt stay on your device and are never transmitted.

This information is saved locally using the operating system’s standard storage. It is not sent to us or to any server in this version of the app.

3. What we don’t collect

  • No advertising identifiers and no advertising.
  • No analytics or crash‑reporting SDKs, and no cross‑app or cross‑site tracking.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or health data.
  • No public profiles, followers, comments or leaderboards.

4. Notifications

If you turn on practice reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device at the times you choose. These are generated on‑device; no reminder data leaves your phone. You can turn them off anytime in Settings or in iOS Settings.

5. Purchases (Svara Plus)

Svara Plus is an optional subscription/one‑time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details. Apple shares only what is needed to confirm your entitlement. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties. There are no third‑party SDKs in the app that would receive your data.

7. Data deletion

Because your data is stored on your device, deleting the app removes it. You can also sign out (which returns you to a local guest profile) at any time.

8. Children

Svara is rated 4+ and is suitable for general audiences. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

9. A note on cultural content

Svara presents mantras, stories and festivals as living culture, with humility, and acknowledges that traditions vary by region and family. Content is for reflection and education — it is not religious instruction, doctrinal authority, or medical advice.

10. Future changes (cloud sync)

If a future version adds optional cloud backup or sign‑in‑based sync, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and any such sync will be opt‑in. Private reflections will remain local and will not be synced.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “last updated” date.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email priyansh.chordia@gmail.com or open an issue at github.com/pri8771/Svara.

This document describes the behaviour of the current release of Svara, which operates fully on‑device with no analytics, no ads and no data transmission.
