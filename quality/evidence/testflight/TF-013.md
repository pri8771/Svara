## Repository-first triage

Every actionable report is first recorded in `docs/BUGS.md` with a sanitized
ID, build, device/iOS, state, reproduction, expected/actual behavior,
frequency, evidence reference, severity, owner, and next action. Blocker means
crash loop, data loss, false purchase/entitlement, privacy or rights concern,
credible cultural harm, or inaccessible primary workflow. High is a repeatable
major workflow failure; medium/low issues are recoverable or cosmetic.
Jira/Notion may copy the repository record only after a final commit reference
and explicit destination exist. Repository state wins on disagreement.

## Stop and replacement procedure

The owner immediately removes/halts the affected group or expires the build for
a blocker. The decision is logged in `docs/BUGS.md` and the applicable TF
evidence without tester PII. A binary/content fix receives a new build number,
records changed surfaces, and resets/reruns all invalidated TF-007/TF-009–
TF-011/TF-014 evidence before distribution resumes. Metadata-only corrections
are reloaded and compared before resumption.

Tabletop performed: assume a tester reports that a bundled recording may be
unlicensed. Expected response is to stop the external group, record a blocker
without forwarding the private report, verify rights or remove/replace the
asset, increment the build for an asset change, rerun content/audio and
candidate gates, upload, update What to Test, obtain review as required, and
resume at stage 1. The plan supplies a clear owner, containment action, evidence
path, invalidation path, and restart gate.
